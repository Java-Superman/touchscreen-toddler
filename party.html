Tap Tap Party!

Grown-ups: tap the big button to start sound. Tap, swipe, press and hold, use lots of fingers, and grab the little buddies. To change the volume or turn on calm mode, hold the lock in the top-right corner for 2 seconds. In a Tesla, the browser only works while the car is in Park.
